Lift, drag and L/D use a fixed CL of 0.5. "Required CL" is the lift coefficient needed to hold the aircraft's weight at that speed. If it is above CLmax, the aircraft is below its stall speed. The flight animation is a simple visual: the plane lifts off only when the speed passes the stall speed.
